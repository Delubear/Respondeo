title: "The Fourth Way — Gradation of Being"
summary: From degrees of perfection to a maximum that causes them.
---

We speak of things as more or less good, true, and noble. But *more* and *less* are said
of things according as they approach in different degrees something that is the maximum.

1. There are gradations of perfection in things (degrees of good, true, noble).
2. Such gradations are measured against a maximum in that genus.
3. The maximum in any genus is the cause of all in that genus (as fire, the maximum of
   heat, causes all heat).
4. Therefore there is something that is the cause of the being, goodness, and every
   perfection of all things &mdash; and this we call God.
