title: "The First Way — Motion"
summary: From the fact of change to an unmoved mover.
---

Nothing moves &mdash; that is, passes from potentiality to actuality &mdash; except by
something already actual. A thing cannot give itself what it does not have; the merely
potential cannot actualize itself.

1. Some things are in motion (they change).
2. Whatever is moved is moved by another.
3. This cannot regress to infinity, for then there would be no first mover, and so no
   motion at all.
4. Therefore there is a first mover, moved by nothing else &mdash; and this all understand
   to be God.

> "It is necessary to arrive at a first mover, moved by no other; and this everyone
> understands to be God." &mdash; *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 2, a. 3
