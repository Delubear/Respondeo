**The objection, at its strongest:**

Even granting an empty tomb and sincere reports of appearances, a natural account is more probable than a miracle: perhaps the disciples hallucinated in grief, mistook the tomb, invented the story, or Jesus never truly died. Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.

*Stub — take each alternative (swoon, hallucination, theft/myth, wrong tomb) on its own terms and weigh it against the minimal facts historians grant.*
