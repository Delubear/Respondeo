# General Roman Calendar - fixed civil-date celebrations (universal/general calendar, Ordinary Form).
#
# This dataset is the single source of truth for every FIXED-date solemnity, feast, and memorial.
# Moveable (Easter-relative) celebrations and Sunday-based feasts of the Lord are computed in code,
# not listed here. Regional/national propers are intentionally out of scope.
#
# Format (pipe-delimited), one celebration per line:
#   MM-DD | Rank | Color | Name
# Rank  : Solemnity | Feast | Memorial | OptionalMemorial
# Color : White | Red | Green | Violet | Rose
# Lines beginning with '#' and blank lines are ignored. Several celebrations may share a date.
