St. Augustine of Hippo is one of the most important theologians in the history of the Church &mdash; a
convert whose searching intellect and restless heart found their rest in God, and whose writings
became a foundation of Western Christian thought.

## From restlessness to conversion

Born in 354 in Roman North Africa to a pagan father and his Christian mother St. Monica, Augustine
pursued rhetoric, philosophy, and the Manichaean sect before a long interior struggle. Moved by the
preaching of St. Ambrose in Milan and a sudden grace in a garden &mdash; *"take up and read"* &mdash; he was
baptized in 387. He tells the story in his *Confessions*.

## Bishop and teacher

Ordained and later made bishop of Hippo, Augustine spent his life preaching, guiding his flock, and
writing against the controversies of his day. His reflections on grace, free will, the Trinity, and
the Church answered questions the Church would ponder for centuries.

## His enduring influence

His *City of God*, written as Rome fell, set the terms for Christian thinking about history, politics,
and the two "cities" of self-love and love of God. A Doctor of the Church, Augustine remains a patron
of theologians and of converts, and his *Confessions* is still read as one of the great accounts of a
soul's turning to God.
