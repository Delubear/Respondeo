**What it is.**
Confirmation completes what Baptism begins.
Through the anointing with sacred chrism and the words "Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit," the baptized receive a fuller outpouring of the same Spirit given to the apostles at Pentecost.
It is the sacrament of Christian maturity &mdash; not because feeling changes, but because the Spirit is given to strengthen and equip.

**Why celebrate it.**
In Confirmation, the Holy Spirit deepens the grace of Baptism, roots a person more firmly in Christ, strengthens the bond with the Church, and gives the courage to live and profess the faith.
It is the same Spirit who transformed frightened disciples into bold witnesses.
To be confirmed is to be commissioned &mdash; no longer only receiving the faith but empowered to carry it.
This is cause for joy: God does not leave His own to face the world on their own strength.
