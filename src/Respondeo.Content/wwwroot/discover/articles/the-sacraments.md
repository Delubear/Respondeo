Everything the Church offers converges here.
When we speak of grace, communion, and the life of God given to human beings, we are finally speaking of the **sacraments** &mdash; the concrete acts by which Christ touches a person through the Church.

A sacrament is, in the classic definition, *an outward sign of an inward grace, instituted by Christ.*
That is, it is a visible action &mdash; washing, anointing, eating, forgiving, joining &mdash; that does not merely *represent* grace but actually *conveys* it.
God, who made us body and soul, deals with us bodily.
He does not leave His grace to float as an idea; He attaches it to water and oil and bread so that we can receive it and *know* we have.

## Why seven?

The Church recognizes seven sacraments because they answer to the whole shape of a human life and its supernatural counterpart:

- Some **initiate** us into the life of grace &mdash; Baptism, Confirmation, and the Eucharist.
- Some **heal** what sin and suffering damage &mdash; Reconciliation and the Anointing of the Sick.
- Some are **at the service of communion**, ordering a person's life toward others &mdash; Holy Orders and Matrimony.

Together they accompany a Christian from birth to death: born in the font, strengthened in Confirmation, fed in the Eucharist, restored in Confession, healed in Anointing, and &mdash; through Orders or Marriage &mdash; given a vocation that pours life back out to others.

## Why celebrate each one

The sacraments are not obligations to be endured but gifts to be *celebrated*, because in each one it is Christ Himself who acts.

- [Baptism](discover/articles/sacrament-baptism) &mdash; the sacrament of rebirth, dying and rising with Christ, washed and made a new creation.
- [Confirmation](discover/articles/sacrament-confirmation) &mdash; the sacrament of the Holy Spirit, sealing and strengthening the baptized for a mature Christian life.
- [The Eucharist](discover/articles/sacrament-eucharist) &mdash; the sacrament of Christ's true presence, the source and summit of the Christian life.
- [Reconciliation (Confession)](discover/articles/sacrament-reconciliation) &mdash; the sacrament of mercy, Christ's forgiveness given personally to the repentant.
- [Anointing of the Sick](discover/articles/sacrament-anointing) &mdash; the sacrament of healing and strength for those facing serious illness, suffering, or death.
- [Holy Orders](discover/articles/sacrament-holy-orders) &mdash; the sacrament by which bishops, priests, and deacons are ordained to serve Christ and His Church.
- [Matrimony](discover/articles/sacrament-matrimony) &mdash; the sacrament by which a man and woman are joined in a covenant that images Christ's love for the Church.
