Perhaps you have followed the path this far &mdash; that God exists, that He has spoken, that Christ rose and founded a Church &mdash; and you find it reasonable, 
even persuasive, but you have never actually *believed* in the way that changes a life, and you have never been baptized.
If so, this step is for you.
You are closer than you may think, and the last stretch is not a longer argument but a different kind of movement.

The reasoning brought you to a door.
Faith is stepping through it.

**What this step is, and is not:**

1. **It is not abandoning reason.**
   Everything you have weighed still stands.
   Faith does not ask you to stop thinking; it asks you to *act* on what thinking has shown &mdash; to trust a 
   Person the evidence has made credible, the way you finally trust a bridge you have inspected by walking across it.

2. **It is a gift, not only an achievement.**
   Christian faith is not merely a conclusion you reach but something God offers.
   You can ask for it honestly &mdash; "I want to believe; help me" is a real prayer, and a good place to begin &mdash; without pretending to a certainty you do not yet feel.
   Scripture itself gives you the words:

   > "Lord, I believe; help my unbelief."
   > &mdash; Mark 9:24

3. **It becomes real through baptism.**
   For someone entering from outside, the turning point is not a private decision alone 
   but **baptism** &mdash; the sacrament by which a person is joined to Christ's death and rising, washed, and made a new creation.
   This is where the whole path has been leading: not to agreement, but to the font.

## See it today: crossing the threshold

You can study a house from the street for a long time &mdash; admire its architecture, verify it is sound, conclude it is truly a home.
But at some point admiring it from outside becomes its own kind of refusal.
The house is meant to be *entered*, and only those who cross the threshold ever live there.

You have examined the house carefully.
The invitation now is not to study it further but to come inside.

**Where to go from here.** Before taking the step, 
it is worth seeing clearly what is actually being held out to you &mdash; the life the Church exists to give.
That is where the path turns next.
Either way, you are no longer a spectator to the case; you are being invited into it.
