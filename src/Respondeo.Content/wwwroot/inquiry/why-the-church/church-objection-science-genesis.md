**The objection, at its strongest:**

1. Science has established an ancient universe, an old earth, and the common descent of living things through evolution.
2. Christianity teaches a six-day creation, a young earth, and a literal Adam fashioned from dust.
3. So to join the Church you must reject the science &mdash; and an honest person chooses the science.

**In response:**

- **The young-earth, anti-evolution package is not Catholic teaching.** It is a recent, largely modern movement &mdash;
  one that grew up outside the Catholic Church and without her teaching authority &mdash; and it does not speak for the
  ancient faith it claims to defend. The Church has never bound the faithful to a literal six-day timetable or to the
  age of the earth.
- **The Church does not require you to disbelieve evolution.** Catholic teaching leaves the biological development of
  living bodies &mdash; including the human body &mdash; open to scientific inquiry. What the faith does hold is that the
  human *soul*, which is spiritual, is created directly by God and is not the product of matter alone. That is a claim
  about what the human person *is*, not a rival theory of how bodies developed.
- **Genesis was never meant to be read as a modern science textbook.** The opening chapters are written in a solemn,
  figurative, liturgical style &mdash; the literary idiom of their own age &mdash; to teach *who* created and *why*:
  that one God freely made all that is, that creation is good, that man is made in God's image, and that sin, not God,
  introduced death and disorder. To force that language into a stopwatch-and-ruler chronology is to misread the genre and
  miss the point the text is actually making.
- **This is not a modern retreat.** Long before Darwin, the Fathers and Doctors warned against wooden literalism.
  St. Augustine held that the "days" of Genesis were not ordinary days and cautioned believers not to talk nonsense about
  the natural world in the name of Scripture. St. Thomas taught that where a passage can be read in more than one way,
  the faith is not to be tied to a reading that observation might overturn. Reading Genesis for its theology rather than
  its geology is the older and more Catholic instinct.

**Faith and reason cannot finally contradict each other.** They have the same Author. God is the source both of the
truths He reveals and of the reason by which we study the world, so a genuine finding of science and a genuine teaching
of faith can never really collide. Where they *seem* to, either the science has overreached its evidence or the Scripture
has been misread &mdash; and patient inquiry, not panic, is the Catholic response:

> "Though faith is above reason, there can never be any real discrepancy between faith and reason. Since the same God who
> reveals mysteries and infuses faith has bestowed the light of reason on the human mind, God cannot deny Himself, nor can
> truth ever contradict truth." &mdash; First Vatican Council, *Dei Filius*

- **The harmony is the Catholic track record, not a recent rescue.** The same Church produced the cathedral schools and
  the university, the friar-scientists and the monastic copyists who preserved learning. A priest, Georges Lemaitre, first
  proposed what we now call the Big Bang. The caricature of a faith at war with science is a story told *about* the
  Church, not *by* her.
- **The real distortion is a symptom of leaving the Church.** The loud modern pairing of Christianity with young-earth
  literalism arises precisely where Scripture has been cut loose from the Church and her Magisterium &mdash; every reader
  left to be his own final authority, and the text pressed into service as a science manual it was never meant to be.
  This is the very problem the Catholic answer to [*Scripture alone*](node/church-objections?section=church-objection-sola-scriptura)
  addresses: a living teaching office, reading Scripture in the light of the whole tradition, is the safeguard against
  that fragmentation &mdash; not its cause. Far from being the enemy of reason, the Church is the reason this distortion
  never took root in her.

So the Church you are considering is not asking you to choose between the God reason reached at the start of this path and
the world science describes. They meet in the one truth &mdash; and it is precisely the Church's teaching authority that
has kept that harmony intact where others lost it.
