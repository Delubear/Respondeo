If Jesus is Lord, a further question follows: did He leave us to interpret His teaching alone, or did He found a visible Church and entrust it with His own authority?

The Catholic claim is that Christ established one Church &mdash; visible, hierarchical, and enduring &mdash; to guard and hand on what He revealed.
This stage walks that claim as an ordered path, one question leading to the next:

- Did Christ found a Church, and give it a teaching office?
- How do we recognize that Church among the many that claim His name?
- What are the marks &mdash; one, holy, catholic, apostolic &mdash; and where are they found today?
- How is His revelation preserved, and who has authority to interpret it?
- Does the claim survive its hardest objections?
- And finally: where has the whole path led?

Begin below, and follow the path from step to step.
