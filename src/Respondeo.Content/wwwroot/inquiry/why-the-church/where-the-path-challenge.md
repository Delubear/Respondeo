If you aren't ready to make the step into the Catholic Church yet &mdash; or if you simply want to strengthen your
faith before moving ahead &mdash; please consider this challenge.
It is a concrete, practical invitation to put the claim to the test in your own life.
The challenge is disarmingly simple: **pray every day going forward.**
It costs seconds to minutes a day, and it asks nothing you cannot sincerely offer from where you stand right now.

> Imagine you're stranded and alone in a dense forest... you have a flare gun. It's super easy to use and it
> takes almost no energy to deploy... I submit to you that it would be crazy not to shoot the flare gun, even if
> you think there's probably no one out there who will see it... What matters is that there's *some* chance, even
> if it's very small, that someone's out there who cares about you and is willing to help you if you ask.
> Likewise... it would be crazy not to pray in your situation, at least conditionally.

A prayer for each place on the road:

- **If you're an atheist or agnostic**, pray this each day, sincerely, with humility and perseverance. It takes
  ten to fifteen seconds:

  > *God, if you exist, please help me to know you, love you, and serve you. Guide me to the full truth about
  > you. If you do, I'll give you my life. And if Catholicism is true, please convert me to Catholicism.*

- **If you believe in God but aren't Catholic**, pray a single decade of the Rosary each day &mdash; about two to four
  minutes &mdash; framed by this:

  > *God, I'm about to pray a decade of the Rosary. If this is against your will, I'm sorry. If this is
  > idolatrous, I'm sorry. You know my heart. I'm doing this solely to discover and follow your will. If
  > Catholicism is true, please make me Catholic.*

  and closing with:

  > *God, I trust that you know my intentions are pure. I seek you above all else. Have mercy on me for my sins.*

- **If you're already Catholic**, learn to pray the full five-decade Rosary and pray it every day. Your day
  should feel incomplete without it.
