Reason can reach *that* God exists and something of *what* He is. But reason
alone cannot tell us whether this God has freely chosen to speak to us. That is
a question of **history**, not only philosophy.

The Christian claim is that God entered history in Jesus of Nazareth — whose
life, death, and Resurrection are attested by witnesses who died rather than
recant. From here the path continues:

- Did Jesus claim divine authority, and vindicate it by rising?
- Did He found a visible Church and entrust it with His teaching office?
- Where is that Church, with its marks, today?
