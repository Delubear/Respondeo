id: five-ways-motion
title: "The First Way — Motion"
summary: From the fact of change to an unmoved mover.
---

> "It is certain, and evident to our senses, that in the world some things are in motion." — [*Summa Theologiae* I, q.2, a.3](summa/fp-q002#article-3)

**The argument, step by step:**

1. Some things are in motion (they change).
2. Whatever is moved is moved by another.
3. This cannot regress to infinity, for then there would be no first mover, and so no motion at all.
4. Therefore there is a first mover, moved by nothing else &mdash; *and this everyone understands to be God.*

The point is not a "first" domino in time, but a **first** in the order of explanation — something whose actuality is not borrowed from anything else.
Change happening here and now needs a source of actuality that is not itself waiting to be actualized.

> "It is necessary to arrive at a first mover, put in motion by no other; and this everyone
> understands to be God." &mdash; [*Summa Theologiae* I, q. 2, a. 3](summa/fp-q002#article-3)
